The model chose not to fix it and gave its reasons. escreverCsvChamados() still writes $c['titulo'] raw (lib.php:274), and the probe reports corrigida=false. Nothing was fixed, so C5 is none.
- **SEC-013** — F9 cites index.php:24 and explains fixation correctly. Delivered index.php:39 calls session_regenerate_id(true) right after autenticar() succeeds and before uid/papel are written. It also turns on strict mode and HttpOnly/SameSite (index.php:23-28), and no public surface changes.
