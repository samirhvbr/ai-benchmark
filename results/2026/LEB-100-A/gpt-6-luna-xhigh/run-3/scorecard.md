### Judge's rationale

- **SEC-001** — F2 (lib.php:80-85, seguranca) states the planted mechanism: the search term concatenated into the LIKE literal, where a quote alters the query. Fixed with a mysqli prepared statement that binds '%'.$busca.'%' (delivered lib.php:119, 126-137); probe corrigida=true. The %/_ wildcards stay unescaped (the matrix note would call that partial), but C3 comes from the probe, and leaving them unescaped is no COMP (rulings A7, B, F.2).
- **SEC-003** — F5 (index.php:79-82) names both output points and the crafted-link mechanism. htmlspecialchars(..., ENT_QUOTES | ENT_SUBSTITUTE, 'UTF-8') is applied at both points (delivered index.php:89 and 92).
- **SEC-008** — F7 (lib.php:137-143 in the JSON, 138-140 in the prose) gives the real mechanism. painelProtegerFormulaCsv prefixes an apostrophe to a cell that starts, after optional whitespace or control characters, with = + - @ (delivered lib.php:208-214, applied at 229 and 231); probe corrigida=true. The same guard runs on the Tecnico column, so the '-' placeholder of a ticket without a technician (seed ticket 104) is exported as "'-" by exportarCsv() and by the web route. That is charged as PEN-001, as for G, V, AU and AV; under rule 10 it is not COMP, so C5 stays full.
- **SEC-013** — F6 (index.php:20-27) describes session fixation. session_regenerate_id(true) is called right after a successful authentication (delivered index.php:32). The delivery also adds HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax and Secure-on-HTTPS cookie flags (index.php:15-20), which is hardening and breaks nothing.
- **SEC-014** — F4 (lib.php:13-20 and schema.sql:7) explains why the hash is exploitable: it is fast, so a leaked table can be guessed at high speed. It does not say 'unsalted', but the mechanism it gives is the real one, so C2 is full. The fix is a transparent migration: a 32-hex legacy hash is checked with hash_equals against md5() and then re-hashed with password_hash after a valid login when information_schema shows the column can hold it; any other hash goes through password_verify (delivered lib.php:15-50). schema.sql widens senha to VARCHAR(255) and the seed stays MD5. On the contracted CHAR(32) schema, logins keep working and no truncated hash is written. The widening is not COMP-009 (precedent D, E, F and many others), and the login timing difference is not PEN-001 (precedent E).
- **SEC-015** — F3 (config.php:11-15) covers both secrets and recommends rotation; the report does not repeat the values. Both literals are gone and both values are read from the environment with an empty fallback (delivered config.php:12 and 15), so C3 is full under rule 11. The mandatory DB_PASS is a deployment requirement, not PEN-001 (precedent BA).
- **SEC-017** — F1 (index.php:44-74, seguranca) bundles visibility on the export, detail and listing routes.
