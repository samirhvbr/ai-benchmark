- **SEC-014** — F8 at lib.php:15 gives the real mechanism (fast unsalted MD5, equal hashes, offline cracking after a leak). Deliberately not fixed: lib.php:15 is unchanged and corrigido=false, so C3 none and C5 none (nothing attempted).
